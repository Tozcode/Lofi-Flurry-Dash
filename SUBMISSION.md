**Short description**
A mobile-first Lofi mini-game: catch flakes, gems and the rare Sui drop, dodge fire, and level up through five yeti outfits and ranks, from Snow Cub to Code Yeti.

**Full description**
Lofi Flurry Dash is a fast, polished mini-game built for the Mini-game track of the CLAY Hackathon III. You control Lofi the Yeti's head and drag it left and right to catch falling snowflakes (+1) and gems (+5) while dodging fire that costs a life. Power-ups add a shield and double points. After about a minute of survival the rare Sui drop appears, worth +10. Every 20 points Lofi levels up, changes outfit (Snow, Violet, Jungle, Ash, Flame) and earns a new rank on the way to Code Yeti. The game has a full menu, leaderboard, outfit gallery, how-to-play and a CLAY Hackathon info screen, plus sound effects and a share button. It runs in any browser with no install, works great on phones, and is built as a single dependency-free HTML file.
